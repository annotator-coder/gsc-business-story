02 — DISTILLATION

Separating crude oil by boiling point
into a variety of fractions

Heated and fed into the atmospheric distillation column, crude oil is separated by boiling point into LPG, naphtha, kerosene, diesel, and residue. Each fraction then serves as feedstock for downstream refining, upgrading, and petrochemical processes.

02 — CAPACITY

0K bbl / day

GS Caltex has a crude oil refining capacity of 800,000 barrels a day. State-of-the-art automated production facilities turn that crude into a wide range of high-quality petroleum products, including gasoline, kerosene, diesel, and jet fuel.

WORLD'S 4TH-LARGEST SINGLE REFINERY

03 — UPGRADING

0K bbl / day

GS Caltex has an upgrading capacity of 275,000 barrels a day. Heavy oil separated in atmospheric distillation is converted through upgrading into high-value light oils such as gasoline, kerosene, and diesel.
