GS CALTEX — GROWTH & PERFORMANCE

SINCE 1967 59 Years of Growth and Change

GS Caltex was established in 1967 as Korea's first privately owned oil refining company. Since its first crude oil refining unit started up in 1969 at 60,000 barrels a day, continuous investment in facilities and technological innovation has extended the business beyond refining into petrochemicals, base oils, and lubricants. Today, the Yeosu Complex operates on a global scale, with a refining capacity of 800,000 barrels a day.

GROWTH — BY THE NUMBERS

Building competitiveness
through investment and expansion

Refining capacity at the Yeosu Complex has increased from 60,000 barrels a day in 1969 to 800,000 barrels a day today. From its roots in refining, the business has expanded into petrochemicals, base oils, and lubricants. GS Caltex is now a global energy company exporting to more than 50 countries. FINANCIAL SOUNDNESS — RESILIENCE

Steadily strengthening
a stable financial structure

GS Caltex manages financial soundness alongside profitability. Its debt-to-equity ratio fell from 111.8% in 2021 to 60.5% in 2025, while total equity rose from ₩11.1 trillion to ₩13.8 trillion over the same period. All three major credit rating agencies in Korea have rated its corporate bonds AA+ for six consecutive years, and this stability strengthens the foundation for sustainable growth.

MILESTONES — KEY STAGES

Branching out from refining
into energy and chemicals

GS Caltex began by expanding its crude oil refining facilities, then broadened its business into upgrading, petrochemicals, base oils, and lubricants. Through continuous investment in facilities and technological innovation, it has strengthened its production competitiveness and built a portfolio in which refining and non-refining businesses are closely connected.
