GS Caltex produces high-quality base oil from unconverted oil (UCO) generated by the refinery's upgrading process. A two-stage hydrocracking process allows flexible production of both Group II and Group III base oils, with more than 70% of the output exported.

BASEOIL 90%+ OF EVERY FINISHED LUBRICANT

ORIGIN

The key feedstock
determining lubricant performance

Base oil is the main raw material in a wide range of lubricant products, from engine oil to industrial fluids. Blended with additives suited to the application and required performance, it is made into finished lubricants for automotive, industrial, and other uses.

Base oils are graded by their refining level, viscosity index, and other properties. GS Caltex produces high-quality Group II and Group III base oils, supplying them in Korea and abroad under the Kixx LUBO brand.

01 — FEEDSTOCK

Using unconverted oil
from the hydrocracking process

Unconverted oil (UCO) from the refinery's hydrocracking unit (HCR) serves as the feedstock. Additional hydrocracking, iso-dewaxing, and other steps then convert it into high-quality base oil.

01 — HYDROCRACKING

Reacting with hydrogen
to strip away impurities

Unconverted oil is reacted with hydrogen to remove impurities and convert it into components suited to base oils. GS Caltex's two-stage hydrocracking process allows flexible production of high-quality Group II and Group III base oils.

01 — ISO-DEWAXING

Raising base oil performance
through iso-dewaxing and other steps

Two-stage hydrocracking, iso-dewaxing, and hydrotreating remove impurities and improve viscosity characteristics and low-temperature fluidity, so the oil keeps its viscosity stable across temperature changes.

01 — HYDROFINISHING

A final refining step
to lock in quality

A finishing treatment with hydrogen removes remaining impurities and improves the product's oxidation stability and quality. The finished base oils are then produced in a range of grades and viscosities according to their quality and properties.

03 — HISTORY

Producing since 2007,
expanding supply to global markets

GS Caltex began producing base oil at the Yeosu Complex in November 2007, at 16,000 barrels a day. Continuous facility improvements and expansions have since brought capacity to 30,000 barrels a day, with more than 70% of output exported worldwide.
