THE FLOW — HOW IT CONNECTS

From refining
to diverse businesses

GS Caltex refines crude oil to produce a wide range of petroleum products, including gasoline, diesel, and jet fuel.

Naphtha, LPG, off-gas, and unconverted oil from the refining process serve as feedstock for the petrochemical and base oil businesses, which in turn supply materials and products for industries and everyday life.

Refining · Upgrading · Petrochemical · Base Oil 01 Petroleum Refining crude oil sourced from around the world into gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, and other petroleum products, as well as feedstock for the petrochemical and base oil businesses. 800K bbl/day · World's 4th-largest single refinery 02 Aromatics Producing aromatics such as benzene, toluene, mixed xylene, and paraxylene from naphtha and other feedstock. 2.8M tons/yr 03 Olefins Using a range of feedstocks, including naphtha, LPG, and off-gas from the refining process, to produce ethylene, propylene, and other basic petrochemicals. 2.3M tons/yr 04 Polymers Producing polyethylene (PE) and polypropylene (PP) from ethylene and propylene, and supplying PP compounds for a wide range of uses. 980K tons/yr 05 Base Oils Producing high-quality base oils, including Group II and Group III, from unconverted oil (UCO) generated by the refinery's upgrading process. 30K bbl/day 06 Lubricants Producing a wide range of Kixx lubricants, from automotive to industrial, drawing on high-quality base oils and accumulated technological expertise. 9K bbl/day
