POLYMERS

Turning ethylene and propylene into industrial materials,
with a polymer capacity of 980,000 tons a year

SCROLL

Basic petrochemicals such as ethylene and propylene undergo polymerization to become a range of polymers, including polyethylene (PE) and polypropylene (PP).

POLYMEROS

ORIGIN

A macromolecule formed
from many monomers

The word polymer comes from the Greek poly, meaning "many," and meros, meaning "part." It refers to a macromolecule formed when small molecules called monomers bond together repeatedly.

01 — MONOMER

The basic feedstock
for polymers

Ethylene and propylene are the main monomers for producing polyethylene (PE) and polypropylene (PP).

01 — POLYMERIZATION

Bonding monomers
into macromolecules

Monomers are converted into long-chain macromolecules with the help of a catalyst. Depending on the catalyst and process conditions, properties such as rigidity, clarity, and flexibility can be tailored.

01 — PELLET

Made into pellets
for easy processing

The product of polymerization is a mass of countless tangled chains. It is extruded, cooled, and cut into pellets the size of a grain of rice, which then go through various processing steps before being used to make film, containers, pipes, automotive parts, and more.

03 — TECHNOLOGY

Decades of technical expertise
behind high-performance and eco-friendly materials

Drawing on R&D capabilities built up over decades in the polymer business, GS Caltex develops high-performance materials engineered for diverse uses and customer needs.

GS Caltex also develops eco-friendly materials that add new value to discarded resources, enabling resource circularity and contributing to the Green Transformation (GX) of the materials industry.

High-performance materials

LFTLONG FIBER REINFORCED THERMOPLASTICS

A composite in which a thermoplastic resin is reinforced with long fibers to improve its mechanical and thermal properties. Its superior rigidity and light weight let it replace metal, and it is used in a range of fields, including automotive parts.

TPETHERMOPLASTIC ELASTOMER

Combines the elasticity of rubber with the processability of plastic. With excellent flexibility, resilience, and moldability, it is used across a wide range of industries, from home appliances to automobiles and robots.

Eco-friendly materials

LOW-CARBON MRLOW-CARBON MR PP COMPOUND

A PP compound produced using mechanically recycled (MR) feedstock from waste plastic. GS Caltex has produced low-carbon MR PP compound since 2010 and is expanding the development and supply of high-quality recycled materials made from automotive, appliance, and household waste plastics. In 2025, it became the first in Asia to earn RecyClass certification across the entire value chain for end-of-life vehicle plastics.

MASS BALANCE CRMASS BALANCE CR POLYMER

A sustainable polymer made with chemically recycled feedstock from waste such as plastic and used oil, alongside conventional feedstock. Under ISCC PLUS certification, GS Caltex systematically manages the amount of recycled feedstock input and the amount allocated to products, contributing to resource circularity and the low-carbon transition while maintaining the excellent properties and processability of conventional polymers.
